Loadstring Generator

Paste a link to a .lua script and get a clean, copy-ready loadstring snippet for your executor.

Only http:// and https:// links are accepted. The URL is never fetched or executed.

loadstring(game:HttpGet("…"))()
  • Quotes and special characters are escaped so they can't break the Lua string.
  • Nothing is downloaded or run here — the snippet only runs in your executor.
Paste URL → copy snippet → run in your executor.
